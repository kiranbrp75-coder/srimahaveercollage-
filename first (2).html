SMC MOODBIDRI, D.K.

Our Campus

Sri Mahaveera College in Moodbidri, Karnataka, features a spacious and scenic 23-acre green campus that provides a serene, rural-focused environment for learning. The campus includes well-ventilated classrooms, high-tech computer and science laboratories, and digital lecture spaces. Its partially computerized central library houses over 44,000 volumes. Students can use the 400-meter running track, outdoor sports facilities, multi-gym, indoor badminton court, 500-seat auditorium, Audio-Visual Hall, and 3,000-capacity open-air auditorium. On-campus amenities include student hostels, two cafeterias, safe drinking water, parking, a Syndicate Bank branch, and a post office.
