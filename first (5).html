About Sri Mahaveera College

Established on July 9, 1965, Sri Mahaveera College, Moodbidri, is a leading public educational hub in Karnataka. Driven by a mission of selflessness and community upliftment, our college highlights include:

  • Rich heritage: Founded by pioneering visionaries Dr. T.M.A. Pai and Sri S.N. Moodbidri to bring higher education to rural youth.
  • Quality credentials: Affiliated with Mangalore University and accredited with an 'A' grade by NAAC.
  • The Mahaveera philosophy: Operates on a no-profit, no-loss basis to help keep quality education financially affordable.
  • Spacious campus: A resource-rich 23-acre campus focused on student mentoring, skill development, and holistic success.
