Sri Mahaveera College

Sri Mahaveera College, Moodbidri, is a leading public educational hub in Karnataka. Driven by a mission of selflessness and community upliftment, the college was founded by Dr. T.M.A. Pai and Sri S.N. Moodbidri to bring higher education to rural youth.
